Ritabrata Dasgupta

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow

I am a geodynamicist at Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1, France. My research explores Earth's interior through numerical modelling, linking deep mantle dynamics to lithospheric deformation and surface topography.

I study subduction dynamics, mantle plumes, and lithosphere–mantle interactions. My current MSCA project, SPIDER, focuses on slab–plume interaction in the deep Earth.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at Yonsei University, Seoul (2022–2025). I received my Ph.D. in Geodynamics from Jadavpur University, Kolkata, in 2022.

My work combines finite element and finite difference modelling with scientific visualization using COMSOL Multiphysics, StagYY, ParaView, and GPlates.
